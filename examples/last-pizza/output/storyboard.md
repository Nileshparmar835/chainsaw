# The People v. One Hungry Guy

The last slice of pizza takes you to court

**Story route** Object comedy • courtroom reversal

**Hook** The last slice sues Dex for eating its father.

**Payoff** A delivery guarantee frees him; his victory snack starts the next case.

## Cast

- **Dex** — the hungry defendant and first-person narrator. Orange hoodie, teal cap, oval eyes, one crooked lace. Voice `am_michael`.
- **Slice** — the furious plaintiff. Pepperoni triangle, basil tie, one missing corner, arched brows. Voice `am_onyx`.
- **Judge** — the judge and oven timer. Copper oven, dial eyes and a black robe; door doubles as the mouth. Voice `am_fenrir`.
- **Jury** — twelve silent garlic knots. Three rows of four braided rolls with tiny eyebrows. Voice `af_bella`.

## 1. Midnight slice court

Cast — Dex, Slice

Elements — pizza box witness stand, brass service bell, plaintiff slice, Dex, neon shop window

Camera — Low counter angle, ease toward the plaintiff at the first accusation.

Dex reaches across a pizza box. The last slice stands up inside it and slides a court summons toward his hand.

- **Dex** My pizza sued me.  
  The lid springs open. Slice pops up with the summons. · thump
- **Slice** [deadpan] You ate my dad.  
  Slice points at Dex. Dex freezes halfway to the box. · none

## 2. Evidence table

Cast — Dex

Elements — empty dinner plate, triangular grease print, red exhibit tag, magnifying glass

Camera — Cut to an overhead exhibit. Crash in toward the greasy fingerprint.

A magnifier follows the pizza-shaped grease print to Dex’s fingerprint.

- **Dex** Exhibit A. My dinner plate.  
  The exhibit card skids onto the wood beside an empty plate. · stamp
- **Dex** [whispering] I licked off the fingerprints.  
  A magnifier sweeps across the grease. The last print vanishes. · swish

## 3. Garlic jury box

Cast — Dex, Jury

Elements — twelve braided garlic knots, three tiers of wood, one basil leaf per juror

Camera — Rack the composition upward through the rows. End on the foreman.

Jurors rise in sequence. The foreman deliberately turns his back on Dex.

- **Dex** I demanded a jury of my peers.  
  Twelve little silhouettes stir behind the jury rail. · none
- **Dex** Twelve garlic knots stood up.  
  Three rows stand one after another, then glare. · thump
- **Dex** [deadpan] Tough room. I had garlic breath.  
  A green breath curl drifts past the foreman; all twelve recoil. · none

## 4. The oven takes the bench

Cast — Judge, Dex

Elements — copper oven judge, heat coils, timer display, judge’s robe, ticket

Camera — Hard cut to a symmetrical front view of the judge; push toward the timer.

The judge opens the oven door. The timer starts, overshoots, and gives Dex an idea.

- **Judge** [shouting] Thirty minutes in the oven!  
  The door opens, showing orange coils and a thirty-minute dial. · stamp
- **Dex** [gasp] Wait. The timer says thirty-one.  
  The last digit flips. Dex looks from the clock to the box. · ping

## 5. The guarantee

Cast — Dex, Judge

Elements — pizza box lid, printed guarantee, late order receipt, teal approval stamp

Camera — Overhead close-up. Track the tapping finger to the printed guarantee.

Dex flips the box lid and invokes the delivery rule. The judge stamps the ticket.

- **Dex** Their box had a guarantee.  
  The lid unfolds, revealing its original printed rule. · swish
- **Dex** Thirty minutes or I go free.  
  Dex taps FREE. A receipt gets stamped RELEASED. · stamp
- **Judge** [sighing] Case dismissed.  
  The receipt bounces once while the oven light fades. · ping

## 6. The next plaintiff

Cast — Dex

Elements — striped bread bag, snapped breadstick, tiny briefcase, fresh summons, Dex

Camera — Match the opening box angle. Rack focus from a snapped breadstick to a fresh summons.

Dex celebrates with one bite. The bitten breadstick already has legal representation.

- **Dex** I celebrated with a breadstick.  
  The breadstick cracks into two pieces in front of Dex. · crunch
- **Dex** [laughing] It already had a lawyer.  
  The bitten end raises a briefcase. A fresh summons lands in the opening position. · stamp
